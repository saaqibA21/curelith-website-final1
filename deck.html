05 · INSIGHT

Everyone thinks patients want another health app. We learned they want a physical wallet card linked to WhatsApp.

App fatigue in digital health is real. Tangible utility wins enduring patient trust.

CONVENTIONAL WISDOM
"Build a 120MB Native Mobile App"

Companies spend ₹150+ CAC to acquire app installs. Patients delete the app 48 hours after recovery. When an emergency strikes at 2 AM, passwords are forgotten and app updates block triage. Result: 92% churn.

THE CURELITH INSIGHT
"Zero-Install WhatsApp + NFC/QR Emergency Card"

A physical card stays in the patient's wallet alongside their Aadhaar and driving license. When scanned or messaged, WhatsApp opens instantly with pre-loaded emergency medical profile. Result: 88% repeat engagement, zero churn.

TIP: The best insights challenge common industry assumptions with ground truth from real users.
