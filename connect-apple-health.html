Add to Shortcuts
3
Automate it Daily
To sync in the background automatically:
  1. Open Shortcuts App on iPhone.
  2. Go to Automation tab → click + (New Automation).
  3. Choose Time of Day (e.g. 9:00 PM).
  4. Select Run Immediately.
  5. Search for and select Curelith Apple Health Sync.

🔒 Privacy First: Your Apple Health data is transmitted securely and directly to Curelith API. We only collect basic stats (steps, heart rate, active energy) for coaching purposes. No medical records are accessed.
