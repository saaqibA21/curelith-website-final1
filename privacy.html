Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

At Curelith, founded by Mohammed Saaqib, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you visit or interact with the Curelith website, our AI assistant, demo interfaces, WhatsApp bot, or medical locker services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Details: Your WhatsApp/phone number (used for secure OTP login).
  • Smartwatch Vitals Data: Health data such as steps, average heart rate, and active calories (retrieved when you link your smartwatch/Google Fit account).
  • Uploaded Health Documents: Medical lab reports, PDF files, and skin images that you voluntarily upload to our Medical Locker or AI analysis tools.
  • Chat History: Transcripts of text interactions with the Curelith AI assistant.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to deliver platform functionality, including:

  • Generating clinical AI summaries and interpreting lab reports.
  • Aggregating and displaying your smartwatch vitals in the Patient Dashboard.
  • Enabling your authorized doctors to inspect health logs during consultation sessions.
  • Preventing spam, abuse, and brute-force attempts through rate limiting.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We value your trust. We do **not** sell, lease, or rent your personal health data to advertisers or third parties.

We may share anonymized text queries or documents with safe subprocessors (such as the OpenAI API) to perform structured clinical interpretation.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement robust security controls (including backend authorization checks, HTTPS encryption, and session token validation) to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction of your medical records.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your locker:

  • You can view and inspect all uploaded files in your Patient Dashboard.
  • You can request the deletion of your account and related medical locker history at any time.

7. Underage Users & Children's Privacy

Curelith is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you are under 18, you may only use this platform under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 18 without verified parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.
